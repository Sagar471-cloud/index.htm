About CJP Background

Origin

On 15 May 2026, Chief Justice Surya Kant compared unemployed youth to "cockroaches and parasites." India's Gen Z flipped the insult. Within 78 hours — 3 million followers. Within one week — 20 million, more than double BJP's audience.

Founder

Abhijeet Dipke, 30, former AAP communications strategist. Built the website using Claude AI and ChatGPT. CJP is not a registered party — it's a satirical protest movement demanding accountability.

Who can join?

Eligibility: be "lazy," "unemployed," or "chronically online." No religion check, no caste check, no gender check. "Voice of the Lazy & Unemployed."
